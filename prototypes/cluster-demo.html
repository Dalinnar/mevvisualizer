What happened

Rules
1. Direction: from the Steps toward the Cap (Region must be on the other side).
2. Step block length = stride. Clusters on the same axis use LCM of strides so caps stay aligned; copies = LCM/stride × cycles.
3. Growth = (copies − 1) × stride.
4. Something is pushed by cluster A when it sits entirely past A's steps (for a cluster: its Region + Steps).
5. Shift = max over pushers of (pusher's shift + pusher's growth), per direction — side-by-side clusters don't add up.
6. Cluster gap: anything beside a cluster and entirely past it sideways (perpendicular to its growth, toward +) is pushed by the gap; same max rule, so row 3 moves 2 × gap. Growth and gap shifts add together.
